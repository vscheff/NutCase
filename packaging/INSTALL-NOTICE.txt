NutCase 0.6.0 - candidate for acceptance testing

Requires 64-bit Windows 10 (1809 or later) or Windows 11 and a separate
64-bit FreeCAD 1.1.x desktop installation from https://www.freecad.org/downloads.php.
Python, Qt, PDFium and the Poppler PDF renderer are included.

Generated drawings are preliminary and require engineering verification.
No engineering approval, tolerances, materials or nominal dimensions are assigned.

NutCase stores settings and drawings under your local application-data directory.
Updates and uninstallation preserve those files. Back up drawings and configurations.

This unsigned candidate has not passed clean-VM or final human acceptance.
See docs/RELEASE_READINESS.md and docs/LICENSING_AUDIT.md before redistribution.
